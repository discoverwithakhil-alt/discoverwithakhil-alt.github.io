ABOUT ME

THE PERSON BEHIND THE MUSIC

Hello! I'm Akhil Karthikeya, an aspiring music director, composer and background score creator.

Music is my passion. I enjoy creating original melodies, experimenting with sounds and exploring the world of cinematic music. I play keyboard and piano, and I love reproducing melodies by ear and developing my own musical ideas.

My creative interests include Indian film music, cinematic background scores, emotional melodies, powerful instrumental themes and musical experimentation.
